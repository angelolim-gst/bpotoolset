            About Gusto Toolset

            This is a lightweight, all-in-one productivity tool designed for focus and efficiency.

            Core Functions:

            • Dynamic Clock: Real-time clock sync with multiple timezones.
            • To-Do & Team Management: Integrated task list and automated coaching alarms.
            • Visual Alarms: Set full-screen alerts for breaks, activities, and custom events.
            • Calculator: With normal, formula, and time-summing modes.
            • Calendar View: A quick-glance monthly calendar with US & PH holidays.
            • WFM Analyzer: Upload Excel schedules for automated PHT conversion and optimal coaching slot analysis via Gemini AI.

            Technology & Credits:

            • Built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6).
            • Fonts 'Inter' and 'JetBrains Mono' served by Google Fonts.
            • Formula parsing by math.js, Excel parsing by SheetJS, and Markdown rendering by marked.js.
            • Storage managed by localForage (IndexedDB).
